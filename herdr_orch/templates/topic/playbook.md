# Playbook

What this Topic's Orchestrator does beyond the generic procedure: its gates, its release steps, its conventions.

## Yours alone

The Orchestrator never decides these. Each goes to the user's Inbox with a recommended answer.

- Spending money: billing, budgets, paid plans.
- Production: deploys, promotions, cut-overs.
- Secrets and credentials: creating, granting, rotating.
- Merging, unless this playbook says otherwise.
